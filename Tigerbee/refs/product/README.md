# Product references

Images 01–09 were supplied by the user during the reconstruction, in that order.
They show the Tiger Beetle / Poisonous Bees frame family and its assembly.

Image 06 labels: 295 mm wheelbase, 5 mm arms, 2.5 mm top board and bottom plate,
7 inch propellers. Image 07 advertises 7/8 inch variants and 5/5.5 mm arms;
these are separate variants, not interchangeable confirmed measurements.

Photos show four arms, overlapping lower plates that clamp the arm roots,
a raised slotted top plate, standoffs, and end brackets. They do not supply
dimensioned standoff lengths, bracket drawings, or an exact arm-type assignment.
Use the existing CAD for original geometry and these images for assembly intent.
